Our Products

Premium halal beef, mutton, and offal — sourced from Pakistan's finest livestock, processed to international standards, and available in custom specifications.

Premium Beef

Halal beef for global markets

Our beef is sourced from healthy, pasture-raised cattle across Punjab and Sindh. Every animal undergoes veterinary inspection before halal slaughter in certified facilities. We supply both bone-in and boneless cuts, frozen or chilled to buyer specifications.

Available in primal cuts, sub-primals, and retail-ready portions. We accommodate custom cutting specifications for institutional and wholesale buyers.

  • Tenderloin
  • Striploin
  • Ribeye
  • Chuck
  • Brisket
  • Topside
  • Silverside
  • Knuckle
  • Shin / Shank
  • Mince (ground beef)

Premium Mutton

Halal goat & lamb for discerning buyers

Pakistan is one of the world's largest producers of goat meat, and our mutton is among the finest available for export. We source from breeds known for tenderness and flavour, with full traceability from farm to facility.

Ideal for Middle Eastern, Central Asian, and Southeast Asian markets where goat and lamb are dietary staples. Available whole, halved, or in custom portion specifications.

  • Whole carcass
  • Leg (bone-in / boneless)
  • Shoulder
  • Rack
  • Loin chops
  • Neck
  • Breast
  • Shank
  • Mince (ground mutton)
  • Cubed / diced

Offal & By-Products

Variety meats for specialised markets

We supply a full range of edible offal and by-products, which are in high demand across many of our export markets. All offal is processed, cleaned, and frozen under the same strict quality controls as our primal cuts.

Offal products are particularly sought after in Gulf, African, and East Asian markets. We can package and label according to destination market requirements.

  • Liver
  • Heart
  • Kidney
  • Tongue
  • Tripe
  • Brain
  • Trotters (paya)
  • Tail
