Our Markets

From the Gulf states to Central Asia and beyond — Safi Meats serves institutional buyers, distributors, and retailers across high-demand halal markets worldwide.

Where We Operate

Serving buyers across six regions

Pakistan's geographic position and established trade routes give us a natural advantage in serving the world's fastest-growing halal meat markets. We maintain active trade relationships and regulatory approvals in each region.

Our largest export destination. The GCC states represent the core of our business, with established relationships across major distributors and retail chains. We hold all necessary import certifications for GCC markets.

  • Saudi Arabia
  • Qatar
  • Kuwait
  • Bahrain

Growth Market

Central Asia

A rapidly growing market for halal protein. Our overland trade routes provide cost-effective access to landlocked Central Asian markets, where demand for quality imported meat continues to rise.

  • Afghanistan
  • Uzbekistan
  • Tajikistan
  • Kyrgyzstan
  • Turkmenistan

Growth Market

Southeast Asia

Home to the world's largest Muslim-majority populations, Southeast Asia represents enormous growth potential. We supply both frozen and chilled products to distributors across the region.

  • Malaysia
  • Indonesia
  • Vietnam
  • Philippines

Strategic Market

China

China's halal meat demand is driven by both its significant Muslim population and growing consumer interest in premium imported protein. We serve buyers in Xinjiang, Ningxia, and eastern seaboard cities.

  • Eastern China
  • Western China

North and East Africa present growing opportunities for Pakistani halal meat exports. We are building trade channels with importers and government procurement agencies across the continent.

  • Libya
  • Kenya
  • Tanzania
