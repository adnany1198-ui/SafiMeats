Farm to Market

Our Process

From trusted livestock suppliers in Pakistan to temperature-controlled delivery at your facility — every step is managed with precision and care.

01

Sourcing

Direct from trusted farms

We partner directly with livestock farmers across Punjab and Sindh, selecting cattle and goats that meet our strict breed, age, and health criteria. Every animal is inspected by our procurement team before purchase.

Our direct-sourcing model eliminates middlemen, ensuring better prices for farmers and complete traceability for our buyers. We maintain long-term relationships with over 50 supplier partners.

02

Processing

Halal-certified slaughter & processing

All processing takes place in certified facilities that adhere to Islamic slaughter guidelines under the supervision of qualified religious authorities. Our abattoirs maintain HACCP and ISO 22000 compliance.

Carcasses are graded, deboned, and portioned according to buyer specifications. Whether you need primal cuts, retail-ready portions, or custom specifications, our processing lines deliver precision at scale.

03

Cold Chain

Temperature-controlled from plant to port

Our cold chain begins the moment processing is complete. Blast freezing at -35°C ensures rapid core temperature reduction, and products are maintained at -18°C through storage, transport, and delivery.

We operate dedicated refrigerated warehousing at our facilities and partner with certified cold chain logistics providers for road and sea transport. Real-time temperature monitoring provides full visibility throughout transit.

04

Export & Delivery

Global shipping with full documentation

Every shipment is accompanied by complete export documentation: halal certificates, health certificates, phytosanitary clearances, and certificates of origin. We handle customs clearance and coordinate with freight partners.

Whether shipping via sea containers from Karachi port or air freight for time-sensitive orders, we manage end-to-end logistics to ensure your order arrives on schedule and in perfect condition.
