About Safi Meats

A premium halal meat exporter rooted in Pakistan, built on integrity, and focused on delivering excellence to global markets.

Our Story

Built on trust, driven by quality

Safi Meats was founded with a singular vision: to bring Pakistan's finest halal meat to the world's most discerning markets. What began as a regional supply operation has grown into a trusted export partner serving buyers across the Middle East, Central Asia, and Southeast Asia.

We work directly with livestock farmers and certified abattoirs across Punjab and Sindh, ensuring full traceability from pasture to port. Our commitment to halal integrity, cold chain discipline, and international food safety standards has made us the partner of choice for institutional buyers who demand consistency.

"We don't just export meat. We export confidence — the confidence that every shipment meets the highest standards of halal certification and quality assurance."
